# Apply a remediation

Use this guide to fix a finding with its remediation steps.

1. Open the finding in the report.
2. Do each numbered step in order. Do not skip a step.
3. Make one change at a time on the device or network.
4. Record a new capture after you finish all steps. Record for at least 10 minutes.
5. Run BACcheck again on the new capture. Confirm that the finding no longer appears.

A short capture can hide a problem. A finding that does not appear can mean that BACcheck had too little evidence. See [Why evidence floors exist](exp-floors.md).
